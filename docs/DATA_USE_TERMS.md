# FSBench-40K data use conditions

These conditions accompany applications for access to materials supplied by the project. The written approval identifies the authorised release, research use and users. Image-level source rights and applicable original licences remain in force.

## 1. Research scope

Use the supplied data only for the research purpose, project and access period stated in the approved application. Describe any intended commercial, product-development or operational use separately and obtain written permission before that use.

## 2. Named users

Limit access to the applicant and approved team members. The responsible researcher oversees use within the project. Notify the corresponding author before adding users or transferring responsibility to another institution.

## 3. Accurate application

Provide accurate identity, affiliation, contact and project information. Use an institutional email where available; otherwise supply an institutional profile or another verifiable research contact.

## 4. Secure storage

Store the data in access-controlled research storage. Protect download links, credentials and backups. Identify any external cloud service or third-party processing platform in the application before uploading data to it.

## 5. Distribution

Do not publish, mirror, sell or pass on the supplied archive, access links or image-level copies to unapproved users. Direct new users to the official application process. Existing permissions under original third-party licences remain unaffected.

## 6. Source rights

Respect image-level source acknowledgements and applicable third-party terms supplied with the release. Access approval does not transfer copyright or grant permissions that the project does not hold.

## 7. Citation

Cite FSBench-40K and the associated HPRM-Former work using the citation supplied with the release, and acknowledge original sources where required. State the release identifier used in each resulting publication.

## 8. Evaluation protocol

Preserve supplied image identifiers and split manifests for benchmark comparisons. Keep test data separate from model selection. Clearly identify custom splits, additional training data, relabelling or preprocessing changes in reported experiments.

## 9. Derived outputs

Report aggregate findings and model results freely. Before publishing sample images or a derivative that contains or reconstructs individual source images, check the applicable release and source permissions. Access does not depend on favourable findings or co-authorship.

## 10. Privacy and purpose

Use the material for the stated visual research task. Do not attempt to identify or contact people depicted, or extract personal information unrelated to that task. Handle accidentally discovered sensitive content responsibly.

## 11. Quality feedback

Report suspected duplicates, label errors, source-rights concerns or unintended disclosures to the corresponding author, preferably with release and image identifiers. Keep a record of any local corrections used in an experiment.

## 12. Operational use

Evaluate suitability independently before any use beyond research. Dataset access is not a certification of a model or an operational fire-warning system; additional validation and permissions may be required for deployment.

## 13. Changes and closure

Request an update when the project purpose, team, institution or access period changes. At the agreed end of access, follow the written decision on retention, extension or deletion of supplied data and copies.

## 14. Administration

Send applications privately to the corresponding author. Application information is used to review the request, administer access and contact the research team. Report necessary corrections through the same contact; do not post completed forms in public issues.

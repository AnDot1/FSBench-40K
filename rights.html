Rights and access

This repository contains the project presentation, showcase figures and blank application materials. A public view of the repository, if enabled by its owner, does not by itself grant redistribution rights to the complete dataset or to third-party source images.

Dataset access follows the application process and the conditions in the written approval. Original third-party rights and licences continue to apply; this project cannot grant rights it does not hold. No blanket software licence is applied to the image dataset.

For permissions or source-attribution enquiries, contact quhaicheng@lntu.edu.cn.

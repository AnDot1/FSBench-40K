FSBench-40K

Foggy Smoke Benchmark-40K

A four-category image benchmark for smoke detection under foggy conditions. FSBench-40K supports distinguishing smoke from atmospheric fog and identifying their coexistence in a single image.

The dataset

Four states. A focused classification task.

FSBench-40K supports the study of smoke–fog ambiguity in individual images, including smoke coexisting with atmospheric fog. The categories separate normal conditions, fog, smoke and their coexistence.

01 / REFERENCE

Normal

Neither visible smoke nor fog.

02 / ATMOSPHERE

Fog

Fog present without visible smoke.

03 / SMOKE

Smoke

Visible smoke without coexisting fog.

04 / COEXISTENCE

SmokeFog

Smoke embedded in a foggy environment.
